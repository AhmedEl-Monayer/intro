1. Computer Definition
• What is a Computer?
  The word “Computer” comes from the word “To Compute” means to calculate.
•       At the heart of all computers, including those that play videos or display pictures, is a system that uses just two numbers: 0 and 1.
        Everything we see on a screen - whether it’s a photo, a video, or a game - is made up of thousands or millions of these 0s and 1s.
•       For example, a picture is made up of tiny dots called pixels, and each pixel's color and brightness are coded in binary. Similarly, a
        video is just a series of images shown quickly, all stored and processed using 0s and 1s.

3. Characteristics of Computer
Why is computer so powerful and widespread?
Computers are powerful and widespread because of their unique characteristics. Here are some key features
that make them so important and commonly used:
1. Speed (‫)السرعة‬
Computers can process information fast, which would take humans much longer.
When you search for something on Google, it scans through billions of data in a fraction of a second to find best results.
2. Accuracy )‫(الدقة‬
Computers are precise and make very few mistakes, especially when performing tasks like calculations or data analysis.
If you use a calculator app on your phone to calculate a math problem, it will give you the exact result, without errors,
even for complicated equations.
3. Efficiency )‫(الكفاءة‬
Computer can complete tasks using minimal resources (like time, or effort) while still producing desired results.
When computer perform a project, it can run multiple programs at once without getting tired.
4. Storage )‫(التخزين‬
Computers can store large amounts of data, such as files, pictures, videos, and documents, and make it easy to access
that data whenever you need it.
  8
3. Characteristics of Computer
5. Automation )‫(التحكم‬
Computers can perform repetitive tasks automatically without human intervention, saving time and effort.
Your phone do required tasks as smart homes control, alarms every day automatically.
6. Connectivity )‫(االتصال‬
Computers can connect to internet and other devices to share information, and access data from anywhere.
At sending message on WhatsApp, it is delivered instantly to the other side of the world.
7. Diversity (‫)تنوع المهام‬
Computers can perform a wide variety of tasks, from word processing to gaming to scientific research, all depending
on the software they are running.
8. Portability )‫(سهولة الحمل‬
Computers come in various sizes, from large desktop models to small, portable laptops and smartphones.
You can carry your smartphone in your pocket and use it with various functions.
9. Evolution )‫(التطوير المستمر‬
Computers constantly improve, becoming more powerful, efficient, and capable of performing more complex tasks with
each new generation.

1. Manual computing
Early humans needed a way to count things like animals or food. They used fingers and stones to keep track of
numbers.
People would use their fingers as the first “calculator” or use physical objects like stones or sticks to represent
numbers. This is the first recorded attempt to count and calculate.

2. Abacus (2400 BCE, Mesopotamia) )‫(عداد األباكوس‬
The abacus is an ancient counting tool that originated in Mesopotamia (‫ )بالد ما بين النهرين‬by Sumerian and later
spread to different parts of the world.
How It Worked: It consists of a series of rods columns, each holding beads (‫)خرز‬. By moving the beads, you
could perform basic arithmetic (addition, subtraction, multiplication). It was like a manual calculator.
The columns represent place values: First column on the right represents ones. Second column represents tens. Third represents hundreds,
and so on. The beads on each rod represent the number of that place value.

-No hard drive or floppy drive, data were loaded into RAM or through paper tape.
-No graphical user interface (GUI); users interacted with it by binary code via panel switches or paper tape.

6.1 Size
1. Embedded Systems (Small Tools)
•    Size: Very small.
•    Examples: Computers inside everyday objects, like your calculator, microwave, washing machine, or a smart watch.
•    Usage: Performing very specific tasks, like controlling a washing machine’s settings.




    26
6.1 Size
2. Personal Computers (PCs)
•    Size: Small to medium-sized.
•    Examples:
      o  Desktop PC: Computer that stays on a desk, with a monitor, keyboard, and mouse. Used at home or in offices.
      o  Laptop: A portable version of a desktop. It’s small and can be carried around easily.
•    Usage: Personal tasks, schoolwork, entertainment, and light office work.




    27
6.1 Size
3. Workstations
•    Size: Slightly bigger than personal computers, designed for specialized work one user.
•    Examples: High-performance computers used for tasks like 3D design, animation, or complex calculations.
•    Usage: Heavy computing tasks like video editing, simulation, or engineering work.




    28
6.1 Size
4. Mainframe Computers
•    Size: Large and powerful, many users.
•    Examples: Big machines used by large organizations, like banks or governments, "data centers“.
•    Usage: Processing large amounts of data for businesses or governments (e.g., banking systems, airline reservations).




    29
6.2 Purpose
1. General-Purpose Computers
•    Purpose: Designed to perform a wide variety of tasks.
•    Example:
      o Personal Computers (PCs): Desktops, laptops, that can be used for browsing the internet, writing
        documents, and playing games.



2. Special-Purpose Computers
•    Purpose: Designed for specific tasks or industries.
•    Examples:
      o Embedded Systems: Computers inside devices like microwaves, washing machines, cars, or
        thermostats.
      o Calculator: A simple tool for performing mathematical calculations.
